Audio credits

Recorded music and village life for Prospect Hollow. Thank you to the musicians and field recordists who made these sounds available.

  • Mining music: “Lanterns Below” — an original two-minute instrumental composed and synthesized for Prospect Hollow. Warm plucked strings, low sustained tones, and soft crystal echoes at 64 BPM. No third-party recordings or samples. Generated by scripts/generate-mining-music.mjs; seamless stereo OGG with an MP3 fallback, mixed to −22 LUFS. Covered by the repository's Prospect Hollow Source Available License.
  • Village music: “Porch Swing Days – slower” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Full acoustic performance, loudness adjusted, with a gentle entrance fade; encoded as MP3.
  • Monument unveiling music (monument-unveiling.mp3): “Thaxted (Holst)” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Kevin MacLeod’s orchestral arrangement of Gustav Holst’s 1921 hymn tune. A 20-second excerpt of the final full-orchestra verse, cut on a downbeat and ending on its held closing chord, with a short de-click entrance, the hall reverb faded over the last 2.5 seconds and loudness adjusted. 44.1 kHz stereo, 192 kbps MP3; reproduced by scripts/prepare-monument-music.mjs. Source and license checked 9 October 2026.
