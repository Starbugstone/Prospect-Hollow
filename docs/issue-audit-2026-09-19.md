# Open issue audit — 19 September 2026

Reviewed all six open issues against the current develop implementation, their acceptance criteria, discussion and available verification. Closing a shipped implementation does not imply that retention or physical-device performance has been measured.

The recorded L01–L10 playtest in #44 exercised ordinary play and town construction; its concrete follow-up findings remain open in #44. Broader human testing remains useful, without keeping the original engineering delivery open indefinitely. |
